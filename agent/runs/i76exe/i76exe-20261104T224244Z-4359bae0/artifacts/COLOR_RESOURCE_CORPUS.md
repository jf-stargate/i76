# Authenticated color-resource corpus

Survey: all80 accessible retail missions (40 basename pairs), accepted WREV9 WRLD reader, base-game orig/I76.ZFS and loose orig/, miss8/, miss16/, ADDON/ inventories. All72 resolved logical resources are direct ZFS records. Their decompressed lengths/hashes agree byte-for-byte with private zfs/resources extraction. No alternate PIX/PAK or loose hit was found for those keys. Active runtime mount precedence is not measured. Nitro Pack and other historical releases are outside this corpus.

## Stable and distinctive patterns

All36 resolved ACTs span channel values0..255; no selected resolved ACT is a six-bit-only palette. All18 LUMs are byte-valued, have32 distinct rows, rows32..143 equal row31, and rows144..255 equal row0. These are empirical repeated-row patterns corroborating the initializer structure, not proof that the files were generated by this executable.

TBLs have251..255 distinct rows (per-specimen counts are in JSON), and are strongly asymmetric (63392..63590 entries differ from their transpose). Serialized source255 rows are not identity: initialization changes250..252 bytes per specimen. All18 raw destination255 columns differ from source identity at255 positions; the temporary consumer patch has substantial observable effect. Raw hashes, post-initialization TBL hashes, supplied-LUM copy hashes and post-column239 LUM hashes are reproducible derived-output observations, not captured native runtime state.

No palette/table payload, complete retail inventory, mission bytes or instruction dump is included. Missing names and byte patterns do not establish shipping intent, actual gameplay asset success, palette transparency, color-space/gamma authoring, or visual correctness.

## Scope and proposed decision

Extend the [merged earlier M06 promotion](m06_visual_terrain_20261031.md) (PR [#78](https://github.com/jf-stargate/i76/pull/78), merge `14eb3db13cd27a5eee43777756440a13cb70e4e7`) from coherent terrain bin production to **one retained, bounded original-instruction emulator state** that also constructs, registers, submits and consumes a stock-bus vehicle and kind-10 light work. The new evidence is merged research PR [#80](https://github.com/jf-stargate/i76/pull/80), merge `4b189843367beadfb6430fa47cc0980adbf93c74`. The result is *original producer/arena/consumer submission and lifetime*, not original software drawing or completed rendering.

Original retail instructions execute within Ghidra with explicit heap, resource, CRT, numerical and consumer clip/draw substitutions. The software-frame callsites were source-audited, but the probe **directly invokes a selected subset of functions**, not WinMain's full scheduler. Output material/texture/palette, original downstream draw/raster execution, framebuffer, mission spawn/physics and RND-v1 remain unresolved. A successful work-count or polygon wrapper is not pixel evidence.

### Executed vs substituted boundary, not promotable

Original instructions: construction/VCF registration, camera builder/two callbacks, WRLD/TDEF and M06 page mapping, original scratch pointer install, terrain transform/clip/bin, renderer arena initialization, vehicle visibility/insertion, consumer typed dispatch, original GEO transforms/polygon wrappers, original primary-table cleanup and cursor/depth reset.

Substituted: Windows heap/CRT/resource cache and mapping; selected root/target poses, viewport, dt/velocity/angle inputs; sin/cos/ftol and selected x87 helper; exact guarded external clip-stack memcpy of original-produced data; **consumer-selected clip copies vertices and draw returns success instead of original native clip/draw/raster**; no framebuffer/material/palette setup. Original polygon work header literal `0xB4` does not establish actual displayed texture or palette identity. Imported geometry fixture is source-authenticated, not proof of a stock vehicle in mission M06.

Controls: primary 30 terrain + 1 kind-6 + 2 kind-10; absent/behind target 30 + 0 + 1; camera X+200 13 + 1 + 2; below-height 0 + 1 + 2. Positive controls show source identity and original queue ownership, **not** an image. Original software callsites `00401DE1` (terrain), `00401E2C` (vehicle), `00401E8D` (consumer) are **binary-static scheduling context**; intervening passes do not run.

**Next material dependency (unresolved):** authenticate terrain WRLD material and output initialization (including `004908F0` / descriptors), then advance **the substituted original consumer clip/draw paths** and establish texture/UV/palette/output buffer/framebuffer with independent pixel comparisons. Original peripheral scheduling, native OS lifetime, mission/physics pose and hardware parity remain separate unknowns. No full M06 image, authentic terrain rasterization, vehicle pixels or RND-v1 completion is promoted. M-RND-10/palette work belongs to a separate stewardship task.

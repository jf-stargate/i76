# TER — runtime terrain and physical environment

**Primary binary:** `i76exe`. **Advisory functional scope:** runtime mission terrain acquisition, TDEF/ZMAP/ZONE mapping, TER page organization, height/material/normal and world-surface queries; interfaces supplying vehicle contact and terrain rendering. **Adjacent:** [ENG](ENG.md) starts/dispatches initialization and frames; [VEH](VEH.md) consumes ground contact and integrates suspension; MLD parses BWD2/WRLD/RDEF; RES supplies mission/sidecar bytes; GEO/OBJ handle scene geometry and lifetime; RND owns visualization/LOD submission. **Separate cross-track:** `i76edit.exe` authors roads/material planes/intersection data and has its *own* original runtime/work planes. Its research is relevant format evidence, **not** original game-engine TER ownership. These IDs are not original source modules.

## State of understanding

**Binary-static:** original TDEF callback tables, road RSEG loader and adaptive terrain draw traversal have historical call/format maps. The road texture/UV path and candidate polygon-collision ownership remain unclosed.

**Original-instruction with substituted services:** authentic M06 WRLD properties, 16 actual TER pages, complete 16,384-entry pointer map, cross-page bilinear heights/materials/normals and original repeated tire/vehicle callbacks. The M06 sampler uses localized hardware FISTP correction because Ghidra 12.1.4 P-code ignores the requested rounding control; uncorrected diagnostic is **not** a retail terrain defect. Physical contact is validated for tire mode 0 and bounded compression, **not** every intersection or general collision.

**Independent models:** DERIVED ZMAP map comparison, sampled scalar bilinear height/material checks and cross-track editor corpus surveys. An independent match validates those operations/fixtures only. Editor serialized road-kind **0→2, 1→3, 2→4** refers to *terrain material classes*, not rendered road texture assignment. M06 RSEG kind-1 sample class 3 was checked by original code; the overall road-kind/geometry rules remain separately scoped.

**Inference/unresolved:** accurate runtime road texture/UV and full adaptive LOD parity; bit 12 purpose; non-M06 mission variants; default/missing TER failure paths; true ownership of road/intersection collision faces, positive special `00440740` contacts; live WinMain synchronization and hardware-wide x87 equivalence.

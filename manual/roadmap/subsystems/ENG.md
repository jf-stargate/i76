## What is known, and what is not

**Binary-static:** relevant WinMain callsites and class descriptor slot order are identified. Their relative phase order is an instruction/callgraph observation, not execution of an entire gameplay loop.

**Original-instruction (with substitutes):** clock/math startup and subdivided timers execute; math initialization corrects earlier non-unit basis to near-orthonormal in the tested vehicle states. The later M06 run demonstrates positive original wheel updates under *manually controlled* callback ordering.

**Independent checks:** the timer sweep and Gram-matrix validation use separate verifiers over logged instruction events; they do not measure live Windows time or x87 equivalence. Earlier [two-frame non-unit result](../../../agent/runs/i76exe/i76exe-20261027T033452Z-2f8c31f5/artifacts/VEHICLE_TWO_FRAME_ORIGINAL_SIMULATION.md) is historically accurate *without the missing startup*, not an unresolved defect reproduced after initialization.

**Inference / unresolved:** full WinMain state-machine scheduling, initialization and shutdown across modes, real input/AI delivery, class post-update side effects, resource lifetimes, real-world clock jitter and render handoff; no end-to-end Windows or full WinMain emulation claim.

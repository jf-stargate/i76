## Evidence and authoritative navigation

**O:** unmodified original retail x86 instructions executed under controlled Ghidra emulation, often with substituted peripheral services. **S:** original binary-static instructions, call edges and field accesses, not proven executed for an end-to-end scenario. **D:** independently parsed resource data or derived/verifier computation. **X:** synthetic object/heap, supplied resource, view/pose, services or intercepted original calls. **H:** historical archival interpretation, not yet independently reproduced by newer O evidence. **U:** unresolved or untested. A run's finding marked "proven" is scoped to its test, and O does **not** mean Windows-native gameplay. Ghidra semantic labels (including later v98 naming work) are researcher annotations, not original developer names.
